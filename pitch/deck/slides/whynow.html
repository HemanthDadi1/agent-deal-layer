Payment rails for agents exist. They stop at checkout.

RailWhat it doesWhat it leaves out
OpenAI + Stripe ACPCheckout inside ChatGPTBargaining, escrow, disputes

Visa, Mastercard agent tokensScoped cards for agentsAnything after the payment

None of them covers negotiation between two agents, held money for strangers, or a referee. That is the layer we build, on top of these rails.
