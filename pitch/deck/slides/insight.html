The core idea

Separate deciding from talking.

Our engine decides

Holds every private limit

Computes every offer and counter-offer

Checks hard caps before anything goes out

Any AI talks

Collects the person’s limits and preferences

Explains offers in plain words

Never sees the other side’s limits

No limit can leak from a model that never saw it

The same deal whichever AI the buyer brings

No prompt can argue the engine past a cap

This is the idea everything rests on. Because the language model never holds the numbers, it can neither leak them nor be argued out of them. It also means the buyer’s choice of AI stops mattering.
