The problem

Agents can already negotiate and pay. Nobody can trust the deals they make.

It leaks its owner’s limit

A step-by-step agent ends right next to its limit. In our stress test, a naive agent offered within ₹100 of the seller’s real floor in 652 of 652 long negotiations.

It can be talked into a bad deal

Microsoft’s Magentic Marketplace study found agents easy to manipulate and quick to accept the first acceptable offer.

A weaker AI quietly costs more

In Anthropic’s Project Deal, people whose agent ran on a weaker model got worse prices, and most did not notice.

Nobody settles the dispute

When a used laptop arrives dead, there is no shared record of the deal, no money held back and no neutral referee.

Sources: Tribunal stress test; Microsoft Research, Magentic Marketplace (2025); Anthropic, Project Deal (Apr 2026).
