The product

Tribunal takes a deal from first offer to final payout.

Any AI comes in: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or a company’s own agent

Through an MCP server, an A2A agent card, an API, or a “negotiate with my agent” link on a listing that already exists.

1 Negotiate

Limits stay private. Price, warranty and delivery traded together.

2 Approve, sign

Both people approve. One signed, hashed record.

3 Escrow

A licensed partner holds the money until inspection.

4 Tribunal

Three judges rule on evidence. Unclear cases go to a human.

No AI alone can bind a person or release their money.
