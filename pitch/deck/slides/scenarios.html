How it feels

Each side states its limits once. Tribunal does the rest.

Buyer

Leaves a standing request

  1. Tells their AI: battery 85% or better, under my limit, within 14 days
  2. Tribunal keeps watching and negotiates when a match appears
  3. Taps approve; the money goes into escrow

Seller

Lists once, stops chasing lowballers

  1. Sets a private floor and what a warranty costs them
  2. Adds a “negotiate with my agent” link to listings they already have
  3. Any buyer’s AI negotiates; the floor is never crossed or shown

Dispute

Evidence decides, not volume

  1. Battery promised at 80%, measured at 71%
  2. The signed record proves what was agreed
  3. Three judges; the refund is the median of the majority; a split goes to a human
