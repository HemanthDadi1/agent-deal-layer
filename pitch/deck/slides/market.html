Trust breaks first in used, high-value goods.

Where trust breaks first

  1. Used electronics: high value, checkable evidence (diagnostics, serials, photos)
  2. Vehicles and two-wheelers: high value, title transfer risk
  3. Freelance and services: milestone escrow already accepted
  4. B2B surplus and refurbished stock: larger tickets, repeat parties

A pilot picks where we start

Candidate A: peer-to-peer resale, India

• OLX India alone: 180 million+ annual users

• Trust is the main reason deals between strangers fail

• Escrow through an RBI-authorised partner

Candidate B: used and refurbished electronics, US

• Big platforms block outside agents; sellers need a neutral venue

• Google tracks prices; nobody negotiates them

Decision gate: share paid online · dispute rate · a partner ready · deal size

Source: HDFC Sky on OLX India (Jul 2025). Market size is sized bottom-up with the pilot partner’s data.

We deliberately have not fixed the first market. Both candidates share the same problem; the pilot data decides. Sizing is bottom-up from deals that need trust, not total resale value.
