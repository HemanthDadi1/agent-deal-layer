Anthropic's Project Deal found people represented by weaker models got worse deals without noticing, and Microsoft's Magentic Marketplace found agents easy to manipulate. Here the AI only states limits; the engine makes every offer.

Without it, each assistant bargains on its own: a weaker model opens higher and gives ground faster (simulated). Seller: BayTech Resale, MacBook Air M2, battery 91%, no warranty.

      

      

      
The strongest assistant does about as well on its own. Weaker ones overpay, and their users never find out. With the engine, everyone gets the same fair price.
